# FairRoom — Intelligent Housing Allocation System: AI Fairness Role

## What the AI-Style Components Support

- Duplicate Aadhaar/ID and old-room detection
- Eligibility and priority-category validation
- Suspicious-data warnings
- Room suitability matching
- Explainable fairness scoring
- Allocation explanation generation
- Report summaries
- Simple resident query assistance

These components are rule-based and explainable in the current project. A trained external AI model is not integrated.

## What They Do Not Do

- They do not secretly favour residents or manually select winners.
- They do not override a stored completed result.
- They do not replace society, builder, government or legal approval.
- They do not verify Aadhaar against an official system.
- They do not guarantee regulatory compliance or eliminate every dispute.

## Why the Allocation Remains Transparent

1. A seed is generated and stored for each completed draw.
2. Resident and room eligibility records identify what was considered.
3. Allocation snapshots preserve names, priorities, rooms and explanations.
4. Audit logs record administrative and draw actions.
5. Completed draw update attempts are blocked.
6. Separate historical CSV/PDF reports and certificates can be reviewed.
7. Draw numbers and references distinguish later allocation phases.

The accurate project claim is: **The system supports fair and transparent room allocation through deterministic lottery logic, validation checks, audit trails and AI-generated explanations.**
